Privacy Policy for Toolzen

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Toolzen is a general-purpose Android utility app. This policy describes the ten-tool launch release (version 1.12.1). Toolzen does not require an account, does not include advertising in this release, and does not offer in-app purchases in this release.

Tools covered by this release

The launch release includes Calculator, Document Scanner, QR Code & Barcode Scanner, Unit Converter, Notes, Password Manager, Flashlight, Currency Converter, Image to PDF, and Image Compressor. Other Toolzen utilities may be added in later versions; this policy will be updated when data practices change.

Information kept on your device

Notes, preferences, favorites, cached reference rates, and other saved Toolzen content are kept in the app's local storage. Toolzen's Android application disables Android backup for its app data.

The Password Manager stores vault entries in an encrypted local file. The encryption key is generated and kept in Android Keystore, and the vault requires the device screen lock to unlock. Plaintext vault entries are not written to disk. The encrypted vault is stored in Android's no-backup app area. Toolzen has no Toolzen account and no cloud vault recovery service.

Images and documents that you choose for scanning, Image to PDF, Image Compressor, or QR/barcode processing are used to perform the requested function. Toolzen does not operate a server that receives those images or documents. When you explicitly save, export, or share output, Android lets you choose the destination. The receiving app or service then handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Camera access

Toolzen requests camera access when a feature needs it, such as QR/barcode scanning or flashlight-related camera hardware access. Camera access is not used for advertising. The release does not request microphone, contacts, location, broad file-storage, exact-alarm, or background audio-recording permissions.

Currency Converter network requests

When rates are refreshed, Currency Converter requests reference exchange-rate data from the Frankfurter API. The numeric amount that you type for a conversion is calculated locally and is not included in the rate request. Frankfurter states that its public API does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs, and that the public instance uses Cloudflare only for aggregate traffic statistics. See Frankfurter's API information.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services

Toolzen uses Google ML Kit / Google Play services components for barcode scanning, text recognition, and document scanning. Image recognition and document processing are performed on-device, although required ML models or scanner modules may be supplied or updated through Google Play services.

Google states that ML Kit Android SDKs collect certain technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics. Depending on the ML Kit feature, this can include device information, app/package information, per-installation or device identifiers, performance metrics such as latency, API configuration, input/output size, feature version, feature event types, and error codes. Google states that this collected data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties. Toolzen does not use this technical ML Kit data to build its own advertising profile. See Google's ML Kit Android data disclosure.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

This release does not compile Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, or Google Play Billing into the release app. Toolzen does not show ads or offer paid utility features in version 1.12.1. Toolzen also does not run a Toolzen-hosted analytics backend. If advertising, purchases, or additional analytics are introduced in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that release.

Feedback and support

In-app feedback is prepared locally. When you choose to share feedback, Toolzen opens Android's share interface and you decide which app or recipient receives it. Toolzen does not automatically upload feedback to a Toolzen feedback server.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the email address and message content that you choose to send. Do not send passwords, vault entries, payment information, or other sensitive secrets in a support message.

Children

Toolzen is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically designed for or directed to children.

Data deletion and retention

Because Toolzen does not create a Toolzen account or upload your notes, vault, or utility files to a Toolzen server, there is no Toolzen cloud account to delete. You can remove local app data through Android Settings or by uninstalling Toolzen. Files that you exported or shared are controlled by the destination that you selected.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when Toolzen's features, third-party SDKs, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.
