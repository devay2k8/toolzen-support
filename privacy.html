Privacy Policy for Toolzen

Effective date: 30 September 2026
Developer: Puneet Dhiman
Contact: puneety2k25@gmail.com

Toolzen provides up to 35 utility tools. Tools are introduced progressively, so some tools described here may not yet be available in your installed version. Additional tools are planned for future releases. This policy applies to the features you can use; describing a planned tool does not mean that it is active or accessing information in your version. Toolzen does not require a Toolzen account.

1. Tools covered and availability

This policy covers Calculator; Document Scanner; QR Code & Barcode Scanner; Unit Converter; Clock (timers, stopwatch, alarms and world clock); Notes; Password Manager; Voice Recorder; Flashlight; Calendar; Currency Converter; Compass; Loan Calculator; Discount Calculator; Random Number Generator; Interest Calculator; Screen Ruler; Text Counter; Dice Roller; Coin Flip; Tip Calculator; Case Converter; Level / Spirit Level; Percentage Calculator; Age Calculator; Date Calculator; Fuel Calculator; BMI Calculator; URL Encoder / Decoder; Image Resize / Crop; Image Compressor; Image to PDF; Protractor; ZIP / File Compressor; and Gold & Silver Price Tracker.

Availability varies by release, device and permission support. Activating a tool already covered here does not by itself change this policy. New data practices will be disclosed before they are introduced.

2. Local information and processing

Most utility operations run locally. Depending on the tools you use, Toolzen stores notes, preferences, favorites, calculation history, calendar events, timers, alarms, calibration settings, cached reference rates, price observations, target alerts, recordings, and selected documents or images on your device. Calculator inputs, text transformations, random results and other utility inputs are processed locally; individual tools may retain history or settings.

Toolzen does not operate a cloud synchronization service for your notes, vault, recordings or utility files. Android backup is disabled for app-private data. Files you export can remain outside the app and are controlled by the destination you choose.

3. Password Manager and clipboard

Vault entries are stored in an encrypted local file. The key is managed through Android Keystore, and vault access uses device authentication. Toolzen does not upload vault entries to a Toolzen server or provide cloud recovery. Clearing app data, uninstalling, or losing access to the encryption key may make vault contents unavailable.

Choosing Copy places information on the device clipboard. Clipboard handling also depends on Android and other applications. Protect your device with a screen lock and avoid copying sensitive information unnecessarily.

4. Images, documents, QR codes and archives

Captured, selected or shared-in files are used for the operation you request: scanning, recognition, barcode reading or creation, image resizing, cropping, compression, PDF creation, or ZIP creation and extraction. Toolzen does not send these files to a Toolzen-operated processing server. Google scanning components are explained below.

When you save, export or share output, Android or the selected application handles the destination. The receiving service's policy applies to information you send there.

5. Audio recordings

Where Recorder is available, microphone access is requested when you start recording. Audio is stored locally and is not automatically uploaded to a Toolzen server. Android recording may continue while you leave the tool or lock your phone, with a foreground-service notification. Use Stop & save to stop recording. You can delete recordings in the app or export them to a chosen destination.

6. Permissions and sensors

  • Camera: document capture, barcode scanning and camera-related flashlight access.
  • Microphone: audio recording.
  • Notifications and alarms/reminders: recording status, timers, alarms and calendar reminders.
  • Foreground services: supported recording and alert playback.
  • Device sensors: compass, level and angle measurements. These tools do not use GPS location.
  • Internet: reference-data requests and supported Google components.
  • Wake lock, vibration and restart handling: keeping relevant operations active, providing feedback and restoring supported scheduled alerts after restart.

Permissions may be declared in a package even when the related tool is not enabled. A declaration does not grant access. Toolzen does not request contacts, GPS location, or broad access to all files. File selection and export use supported Android interfaces. Manage permissions in Android Settings; denied access may prevent a corresponding feature from working.

7. Exchange-rate and metal-price services

Currency Converter requests reference rates from Frankfurter. Conversion amounts are calculated locally and are not included in rate requests.

Where available, Gold & Silver Price Tracker requests quotations from Gold-API and may request currency rates from Frankfurter. Requests can reveal the selected metal or currency; target-price settings are evaluated locally.

These services and their infrastructure providers receive network requests, which expose information such as IP addresses and request details. Their processing and retention are governed by their own policies.

8. Google components and technical data

Toolzen uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services for supported scanning and text recognition. Recognition and document processing are performed on-device, although required models or modules may be downloaded or updated through Google Play services.

Google documents ML Kit technical data collection for diagnostics and usage analytics. Depending on the component, this includes device/app information, installation or device identifiers, performance measurements, feature configuration, input/output sizes, feature events and errors. Google states that the listed ML Kit data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. See Google's ML Kit disclosure.

9. Advertising, purchases and analytics

Advertising and in-app purchase features are disabled in the releases currently covered by this policy. Their packages retain Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform and Google Play Billing libraries; those libraries have not been removed from the app.

Toolzen does not operate its own analytics backend. Third-party components may process technical information under their documented practices. See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

If advertising, purchases or additional data processing are enabled, this policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before the affected release.

10. Feedback and support

Feedback drafts are prepared locally. Email support opens your email application with the support recipient, subject and message. You decide whether to send; opening the composer does not send an email automatically. Toolzen does not automatically submit drafts to a Toolzen feedback server.

If you send support email, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you include. Your email provider also processes the communication. Support correspondence is used to respond, investigate reported problems and maintain relevant support records. Do not include passwords, vault contents or other sensitive secrets.

11. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it using available app controls, clear app data or uninstall. Exported files may remain and must be deleted separately. Toolzen has no cloud account to delete.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and maintain relevant records. Email support to request deletion of correspondence associated with your address, subject to applicable retention obligations. Deleting Toolzen data does not delete content held by services you separately used to share, export or email information.

12. Children and security

Toolzen is a general-purpose utility app and is not specifically directed to children. Contact support if you believe a child has submitted personal information through support correspondence.

Local processing, app-private storage and encrypted vault storage reduce exposure, but no storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Review destinations before sharing sensitive information.

13. Changes and contact

This policy is designed to cover progressive releases of the tools listed above. Changes in tool count alone do not require a rewrite when data practices stay the same. Material changes to collection, sharing, services or purposes will be reflected here and in applicable store disclosures before release. The effective date will be revised when the policy changes materially.

For privacy questions, support or deletion requests, contact Puneet Dhiman at puneety2k25@gmail.com.
